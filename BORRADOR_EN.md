### Intro (3 párrafos)

Most people looking for an **ad agency near me** don't want a national firm with account managers who rotate every quarter. They want someone local who picks up the phone, knows the Treasure Coast, and treats a $600 monthly budget with the same care as a $6,000 one. That's what we do — you talk directly to the person building and managing your campaigns. No layers, no middlemen.

Advertising for a local business is not only digital. A well-run Google Ads campaign brings people who are searching right now; a Facebook or Instagram ad reaches people before they search; and a well-designed business card or a banner at a community event keeps your name in front of people in the real world. A real **advertising company** treats all of it as one plan, not separate silos.

One thing worth doing before you spend a dollar on ads: make sure your Google Business Profile is claimed and complete. It's a free listing in Google Maps that brings in calls at no cost, and we'll tell you honestly if fixing that is the cheaper first move for your business. From there — whether you need paid ads, print, or a mix — we work with restaurants, contractors, clinics, salons, retailers, and independent professionals across Vero Beach, Fort Pierce, Port St. Lucie, and Stuart to put the budget where the customers are.

### Sección: Advertising by city

**Vero Beach**
Advertising in Vero Beach means reaching a market with money to spend and high expectations for how a business looks. Indian River County shoppers compare options carefully before they call, so an advertising company here has to make a small business look as solid as the national brands it competes with — consistent creative across Google and social, and print that matches. We work with Vero Beach boutiques, restaurants, medical and dental practices, and home-service businesses on advertising that fits that standard and still brings in the right customer at a fair cost.

**Fort Pierce**
As an advertising agency in Fort Pierce, we build local advertising and digital advertising around St. Lucie County's mix of waterfront businesses, trades, food spots, and community services. Fort Pierce customers tend to trust businesses that feel part of the neighborhood, so we pair tightly targeted Google and Meta campaigns with printed materials for downtown events, the farmers market, and route work. It's also the city where our own organic search visibility is strongest, so paid advertising here builds on ground we already know well.

**Port St. Lucie**
Port St. Lucie is the largest and fastest-growing city on the Treasure Coast, with new neighborhoods and new competitors every year. As an advertising agency working here, we keep local advertising — Google Ads, Meta ads, and print — pointed at the specific parts of the city where your customers actually live, instead of paying to reach the whole metro.

**Stuart**
We also cover Stuart and the rest of Martin County. If your business is in Stuart, the same services apply — get in touch and we'll put together a plan for your area.

### Sección: Why a local advertising agency instead of a national one

A national agency treats your account as one row in a system. The campaign comes from a template, your questions go to a ticket queue, and no one on the team has driven US-1 or stood in your parking lot. A local advertising agency knows which street festivals are worth a booth, which ZIP codes to target and which to skip, and how a Vero Beach customer reads an ad differently from a Fort Pierce one.

It also means you can reach a person. A lot of **advertising services online** are really just a self-serve dashboard — the **small business internet advertising** customer is left to set the targeting, guess at the budget, and interpret the reports alone. We do that work and then explain it in plain terms: how many people saw the ad, how many contacted you, and what each contact cost.

We're a small team, so we take on a limited number of clients and stay with them. You get experienced attention on a small-business budget, in English or Spanish, with no long-term contract.

### FAQ (4 acordeones `<details>`)

**How much does it cost to hire an advertising agency for a small business?**
For most small businesses on the Treasure Coast, our management fee plus a working ad budget starts in the range of a few hundred dollars a month, and it scales with what you put behind the ads. Print jobs are quoted on their own. Before you commit, we show you exactly what goes to management and what goes to Google or Meta, and we work month to month with no long-term contract.

**What is the difference between advertising on Google and on Facebook?**
Google advertising reaches people who are already searching for what you sell, so the intent is high and they're close to acting. Facebook and Instagram advertising reaches people who match your ideal customer but aren't searching yet, so it builds awareness over time. Most local businesses do best with some of both — our Google Ads and Meta Ads pages explain each in detail.

**Do you do print advertising as well as digital?**
Yes. Business cards, flyers, banners, door hangers, and signage are part of what we offer, and we design them to match your digital ads so everything looks like one brand. See our print materials page for the full list.

**Do I need a Google Business Profile before I pay for ads?**
It's not required, but it usually pays to sort it out first. A claimed, complete profile shows up in Google Maps and brings in calls for free, which can cover part of what you'd otherwise spend on ads. If your profile is thin or unclaimed, we'll often suggest fixing that before turning on paid campaigns.
